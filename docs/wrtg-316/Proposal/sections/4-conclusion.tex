\section{Conclusion}

I ask you to approve this topic and to expect a research paper that argues, from published
evidence, that a robot arm should be judged by task-level tests rather than by its price or
its datasheet, and that names those tests. The question matters beyond this class. Labs are
deciding now which arms to buy, and an affordable arm only opens SDL research to more labs if
someone can show that it does the work. For me, the paper is the foundation of next
semester's research, when I will run these tests on our PiPER and SO-101 arms. For its
readers, it will offer what I could not find when I started: a clear way to decide whether a
low-cost arm is good enough for the job in front of it.
