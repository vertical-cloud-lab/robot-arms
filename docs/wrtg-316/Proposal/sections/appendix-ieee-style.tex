\section*{Appendix: IEEE Citation Style}

This proposal follows the IEEE Reference Guide \cite{ieeeguide}. The table summarizes the
rules it uses, with an example of each from this proposal.

\begin{center}
\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.45\linewidth}
    >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
  \toprule
  Rule & Example from this proposal \\
  \midrule
  Cite a source by its number in square brackets, inside the sentence's punctuation.
    & ``\ldots\ ran 688 experiments \cite{burger2020}.'' \\
  Number sources in the order they are first cited, and reuse the number every time the
    source is cited again.
    & Burger \etal\ is cited first, so it is \cite{burger2020} throughout. \\
  Bracket each number of a group separately; never write a range.
    & Write \cite{harazono2024}, \cite{rupp2022}; never ``[1]--[4]'' \\
  In the text, use ``\etal'' for a source with three or more authors.
    & Zhou \etal\ \cite{zhou2025} \\
  In the reference list, give initials before surnames and list up to six authors; for
    more than six, give the first author and ``\etal''
    & Reference \cite{zhou2025} lists all five authors; \cite{burger2020} has fourteen,
    so it gives ``B.~Burger \etal'' \\
  Journal article: author, ``title,'' \emph{abbreviated journal}, vol., no., pp., month
    year, doi.
    & Tom \etal\ \cite{tom2024} \\
  Journal article with an article number instead of pages: \ldots, vol., no., month year,
    Art.~no., doi.
    & Harazono \etal\ \cite{harazono2024}; Zhou \etal\ \cite{zhou2025} \\
  Conference paper: author, ``title,'' in \emph{abbreviated proceedings}, city, country,
    month year, pp., doi.
    & Quigley \etal\ \cite{quigley2011}; Ferraguti \etal\ \cite{ferraguti2019} \\
  Online manual: organization, city, country. \emph{Title}. (year). Accessed: date.
    [Online]. Available: URL
    & The IEEE Reference Guide \cite{ieeeguide} \\
  Put article titles in quotation marks in sentence case; set journal and conference names
    in italics and abbreviate them, except one-word titles.
    & \emph{Chem. Rev.}\ \cite{tom2024}; \emph{Nature} \cite{burger2020} \\
  End each reference with a period, unless it ends with a URL.
    & Reference \cite{vincent2024} ends ``\ldots 3445635.''; \cite{ieeeguide} ends with its
    URL \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
