# Final selection

The nine sources chosen in [PR #90](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/robot-arms/pull/90#issuecomment-7228945256),
plus two found by hand, in IEEE style. [`selection.md`](selection.md) has the checked quotes and why each of the nine
was picked.

## Review of Literature

[1] Z. Zhou, S. Veeramani, F. Munguia-Galeano, H. Fakhruldeen, and A. I. Cooper, "Localization, inspection, and
reasoning (LIRA) module for autonomous workflows in self-driving laboratories," *Commun. Chem.*, vol. 8, no. 1,
Art. no. 384, Nov. 2025, doi: 10.1038/s42004-025-01770-1.

[2] S. Chajón, J. Reiff-Stephan, and N. Günther, "Photogrammetric characterization of robot positioning accuracy and
repeatability," *Robotics*, vol. 15, no. 5, Art. no. 86, Apr. 2026, doi: 10.3390/robotics15050086.
