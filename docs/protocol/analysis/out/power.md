## H3b: correction widens single-vial ED90 (x and y)

The registered rule (`prereg_stats.h3b_axis_supported`: delta-method bound on the radius difference, Fieller if the corrected curve is censored), on both axes. The last two rows have no true difference, so they are false-support rates.

| Scenario | Power |
|---|---|
| OL pocket ED90 0.3 mm, AT ED90 3.0 mm | 100 % |
| OL p(0) = 80 %, AT ED90 3.0 mm | 100 % |
| OL ED90 0.3 mm, AT catches all to 4 mm | 100 % |
| OL ED90 0.3 mm, AT ED90 1.0 mm | 100 % |
| OL ED90 0.3 mm, AT ED90 0.6 mm | 3 % |
| no effect: both ED90 0.3 mm | 0 % |
| no effect: both ED90 0.3 mm, OL sharper (ED50 − ED90 0.3 vs 1.0 mm) | 1 % |

Why not a percentile bootstrap: the rule first registered in v2, against the registered one (200 simulated campaigns each). The bootstrap detects more in the 0.6 vs 0.3 mm case, but when the two curves share an ED90 and differ in slope it claims a difference far more often than 5 %.
