# Power analysis: results tables

Generated by `power.py`. H1, H3a and D11's E1 clause are exact. Simulated: H2 and H3b with 2,000 campaigns per scenario, H3c with 500, H4a with 500 sessions per row and H4b/H4c with 300, the H3b bootstrap comparison with 200, the H2 refutation-level check with 10,000, the H4a rejected-rule check with 500, and D11's window clause and E2 clause with 5,000 and 20,000. Every simulated decision is made by the registered function in `prereg_stats.py`, and seeds are fixed. H3a's exact power uses the Boschloo test's critical region, computed here and checked against `scipy.stats.boschloo_exact` (which `prereg_stats.boschloo_greater` calls) below.

| OL pocket ED90 0.3 mm, AT ED90 3.0 mm | 100 % |
| OL p(0) = 80 %, AT ED90 3.0 mm | 100 % |

| OL ED90 0.3 mm, AT ED90 1.0 mm | 100 % |

Why not a percentile bootstrap: the rule first registered in v2, against the registered one (200 simulated campaigns each). The bootstrap detects more in the 0.6 vs 0.3 mm case, but when the two curves share an ED90 and differ in slope it claims a difference far more often than 5 %.
