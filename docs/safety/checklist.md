# PiPER cell: checklist for the person at the arm (DRAFT)

Print this one page and post it by the E-stop. The details are in the [SOP](sop.md).
**The arm can move at any time. Remote users and AI agents can start it.**
**Until the E-stop is installed, "press the E-stop" means unplug the arm's cord.**
`[unverified]` numbers are still to be confirmed (see the [README](README.md#how-numbers-are-marked)).

**Start of session**
- [ ] E-stop within reach and unobstructed. Its location is marked on the layout
- [ ] Arm folded at rest. Wrist LED off. Teach-button cover on
- [ ] Cords undamaged and off the floor. Brick outside the dome and uncovered
- [ ] Transformer zone empty: nothing within 190 mm (7.5 in) `[unverified]` and nothing on top
- [ ] Nobody in the reach or under the dome. Lab channel told you're at the arm, and who the remote operator is

**Before your hand goes in (every reset)**
- [ ] Arm at rest and still. If it's raised, wait for it to park. Don't cut power unless it's an emergency
- [ ] **Press the E-stop.** Keep fingers off the wrist and J2 motor: the wrist can settle onto it
- [ ] Fingers out of the gripper and the elbow fold. Don't touch the wrist button
- [ ] Never reach into the transformer zone, even for a dropped vial. Tell the PI instead
- [ ] Hands, tools and loose items out. Flap down

**Before every powered run ("workspace clear")**
- [ ] Nobody in the reach. Flap down. Arm at rest
- [ ] You're at the E-stop, outside the reach, and you'll stay there for the whole run
- [ ] Twist the E-stop out, say "arm power on", and press START. Wrist LED off (flashing = press the E-stop)
- [ ] Give "workspace clear" for **this run only**, with your name. A late start or re-run needs a new one
- [ ] Safety glasses on if you're within about 1 m (proposed)

**During the run**
- [ ] Watch the arm. Press the E-stop if anyone enters the reach, or if the arm heads somewhere unplanned (out of the dome, toward the shelf edge or the transformer)
- [ ] Nobody lifts the flap

**If something goes wrong**
- [ ] Hurt or pinned: press the E-stop and support the arm (it drops). Move it by hand to free them. Call **911** and University Police **801-422-2222**
- [ ] Unexpected motion: step out of the reach first, then press the E-stop
- [ ] Smoke or a smell at the arm: press the E-stop and unplug if it's safe. Fire: alarm, evacuate, 911
- [ ] Anything wrong at the transformer: stay away from it and keep others back. Call Physical Facilities ________ and the PI
- [ ] After an unplanned stop: leave the E-stop pressed and follow the restart steps in [SOP §G](sop.md#g-restart-after-an-e-stop). Log it and tell the PI

**End of session**
- [ ] Operator confirms: arm at rest, motors off, no job running
- [ ] Arm folded, LED off, transformer zone and floor clear
- [ ] Left on for remote use only if folded and limp at rest. Otherwise turn it off
- [ ] Stops, near misses and anything odd logged
