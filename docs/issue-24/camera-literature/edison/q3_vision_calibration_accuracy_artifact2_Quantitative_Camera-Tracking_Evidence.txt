| **Wang et al. (2023)** — binocular camera; ABB IRB-1200; three 25.4 mm ceramic spheres; camera-to-end-effector distance approximately 0.5–1.0 m (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000032) | Across 40 commanded motions of 50–100 mm: mean discrepancy **0.7873 to 0.2604 mm** after kinematic calibration; maximum **1.998 to 0.7107 mm** (pqac-00000033) | Strong short-range stereo result, but the camera measurement defines the observed motion; this is **not independently verified vision-localization error**. |
| **Wakiuchi et al. (2026)** — one fixed camera at approximately 0.22 m; 35 mm planar AprilTag versus 35 mm non-coplanar four-tag fiducials (pqac-00000030) | At fixed frontal orientation over a translation grid: planar-tag positional RMSE **1.50 mm**; non-coplanar four-tag designs **0.17–0.20 mm** (pqac-00000014) | Demonstrates near-frontal planar-tag ill-conditioning, not absolute accuracy: the reference was robot-commanded motion, and a constant transform was fitted to the evaluated data (pqac-00000049). |
| **Sideris et al. (2026), LARK — planar grid** — five Raspberry Pi global-shutter cameras in a pentagon on a 0.72 m fixture, approximately 1 m above the target; 24 mm ArUco tags on a dodecahedron (pqac-00000019, pqac-00000054) | Adaptive-triangulation **target-registration RMSE median**: 2 cameras **0.9584 mm**, 3 **1.0000 mm**, 4 **0.9030 mm**, 5 **0.7477 mm**. With five cameras, the median individual point error was **0.64 mm** (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000011). | Five cameras achieved submillimetre grid performance, but improvement with camera count was not monotonic: configuration and ray geometry mattered. The 0.7477 and 0.64 mm values summarize different units—trial/configuration RMSEs and individual target-point errors, respectively. |
| **Sideris et al. (2026), LARK — head phantom** — same hardware under greater depth variation, oblique views, and partial occlusion | One-camera monocular target-registration RMSE median **2.7304 mm**; adaptive triangulation: 2 cameras **1.7163 mm**, 3 **1.6082 mm**, 4 **1.3957 mm**, 5 **1.2774 mm**. Five-camera median individual point error: **1.13 mm** (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000012). | Accuracy improved consistently from two to five triangulating views, but realistic geometry eliminated the grid’s submillimetre result. Laboratory grid accuracy therefore should not be treated as whole-workspace absolute accuracy. |
