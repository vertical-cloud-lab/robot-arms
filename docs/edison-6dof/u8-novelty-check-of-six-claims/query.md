Context. From a review of recent literature we have drafted six claims of novelty for projects we may pursue in 2027. Before we build on them, we need each one tested against prior work as hard as possible, including preprints, theses, conference papers, technical reports and industrial case studies.

Question. For each claim below, find the closest prior work (any year, any field), state whether the claim survives as written, needs narrowing (and how), or is false, and give the evidence.
C1. No published study reports per-operation handling times with their spread, together with human intervention rates, measured across the hands-on workflow of a materials or alloy development loop (for example atomization, powder workup and characterization), and none validates a discrete-event simulation of a self-driving lab against such measurements.

For each claim give the closest three to five works with DOI or arXiv ID and a quoted sentence showing what they did, and say plainly where no evidence was found. Flag preprints and non-peer-reviewed sources.
