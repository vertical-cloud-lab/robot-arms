## 0. Bottom line for the arm decision

1. **The lab's own schedule says the bottleneck is the hands-on work after each atomizer run, not the atomizer itself.** "The atomizer isn't the bottleneck; the work after each run is. One run takes about **10–15 person-hours** from charge to SEM" (12–19 h with machined cups) ([#256, Nov 24, schedule written Nov 12](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/256#issuecomment-7123300072)). Those hours go to dosing and packing, machining cups, the mass balance, sieving, flowability tests, SEM stubs and image analysis ([schedule README, time-per-run table](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/claude/issue-256-20261112-2149/al-campaign-schedule/README.md#what-one-run-costs-in-time)). Most of that is benchtop, room-temperature, gram-to-kilogram handling: a good PiPER fit.
2. **The critical path right now is the atomizer, and an arm can't fix it.**
   - The atomizer has been "down since the Nov 13 cup failure (#269), and the five Al runs before it made little or no powder" ([#277, Nov 27](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/277#issuecomment-7226073050)).
   - Only 2 of 10 logged runs were good, at 1.7 runs a week ([#276 audit, Nov 26](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/276#issuecomment-7180754041)).
   - Only the three trained operators may touch the machine, and "one slip with the transducer could cost the lab $5-10K" ([#161, Oct 7](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/161); [#160, Oct 7](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/160)).
   - **Keep the arm out of the atomizer chamber.**
3. **Timing window: January to early February 2027.** The plan has about 20 Al runs from Jan 4 to Feb 9 (W8–W13), then a data freeze on Feb 12 and submission on Feb 25 ([schedule README, runs per week and gates](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/claude/issue-256-20261112-2149/al-campaign-schedule/README.md#the-36-al-runs)). The atomizer restart target is Dec 11–18 ([#277](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/277#issuecomment-7226073050)), so a powder-workup cell that works in December on existing lots (bronze `k7m2q9`, the Al lots) would be ready for that block.
4. **The PI's framing favours targeted robotics plus provenance.**
   - FLAIME plans "software-dispatched SOPs [that] map human handoffs 1:1 to Phase II robotic points" ([proposal L902–908](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/genesis-mission-proposal/flaime-genesis-proposal.tex#L902-L908)).
   - The powder lab and the Microscopy Center "are connected directly by a door with no public access, making the layout amenable to integration into robotic sample transfer workflows in Phase II" ([L1189–1191](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/genesis-mission-proposal/flaime-genesis-proposal.tex#L1189-L1191)).
   - The Nov 26 analysis advises "robotics only where their bottleneck is manual work" ([#283](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/283#issuecomment-7182172180)). `sgbaird` stresses provenance and "Development of people as a product" ([#283, Nov 26](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/283#issuecomment-7182240987)).
5. **The biggest safety constraint is combustible Al powder, not the arm's force.** DAL's own guidance keeps unrated electronics *out* of the powder enclosure: "Aluminum is a combustible metal under NFPA 484 (now folded into NFPA 660) … wants dusttight enclosures" ([#150, Sep 17](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/150#issuecomment-6353383317)). "BYU lists no program for compressed gas, combustible dust, oxygen deficiency or robots" ([PR 266, Nov 20](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/266#issuecomment-7051629464)). A PiPER cell should handle closed or low-dust containers outside the enclosure, with grounded tooling, after a review by BYU's College of Engineering industrial hygienist.
6. **DAL has no PiPER or robot-arm work yet.**
   - The only arm work is simulation: UR10e/UR12e plus a Robotiq 2F-85 on a MiR250 tending the desktop LPBF design ([PR 266](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/266#issuecomment-7041099336)).
   - The gantry work is the CubXL corrosion build and a doser concept, neither built yet ([PR 264](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/264); [PR 241](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/241)).
   - `sgbaird` did ask that the lab-wide data inventory count the robot-arms tests: "Also lots of tests with robot-arms" ([PR 288, Nov 26](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/288#issuecomment-7200877676)). It now records "about 30 logged runs on the real PiPER (pick-and-place stages, power and E-stop tests), 11 camera calibrations, and 15 agent-run policy episodes in simulation" ([PR 288](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/288#issuecomment-7200915006)).

## 1. Goals

### What FLAIME, BOFN and pKGFN are
- **FLAIME** = "Fast Learning AI for Mineral Efficiency: Autonomous Discovery and Qualification of Critical-Mineral-Lean Alloys for Clean Energy" ([proposal L148–150](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/genesis-mission-proposal/flaime-genesis-proposal.tex#L148-L150)). It was written for DOE Genesis Mission Phase I, Topic 3B (Critical Minerals, AMMTO), DE-FOA-0003612, due Apr 28, 2026 ([grant-targeting-analysis.md](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/grant-targeting-analysis.md)). DAL doesn't record the outcome.
- **What the loop covers.** FLAIME is an AI closed loop over Al alloys for laser powder bed fusion (LPBF). It runs CALPHAD → ultrasonic (and gas) atomization → LPBF → staged specimens: single tracks, density cubes with a ≥99 % density gate, and ASTM E8 sub-size tensile bars ([L256–263](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/genesis-mission-proposal/flaime-genesis-proposal.tex#L256-L263)).
- **The 10× claim.** The proposal promises "reducing powder-to-tensile cycle time by an estimated 10× … (≥3× from AI-guided experiment selection × ~3× from closed-loop execution speedup) — software-orchestrated with human operators in Phase I, architected for direct robotic integration in Phase II" ([L265–269](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/genesis-mission-proposal/flaime-genesis-proposal.tex#L265-L269)). Phase II "replaces each Phase I human handoff with a robotic endpoint" ([L661](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/genesis-mission-proposal/flaime-genesis-proposal.tex#L661)).
- **Search space.** A 15-element palette over Al, giving 22,803 sub-systems ([L161–199](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/genesis-mission-proposal/flaime-genesis-proposal.tex#L161-L199)). The feedstock list covers Al, Mn, Cr, Zr, Mg, Si, Cu, Ti, Fe, Ni, Ce, Sc, Li, Er, Zn and Sn ([#146, Jul 15](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/146)).
- **BOFN** (Bayesian optimization over function networks) and **pKGFN** (partial knowledge gradient for function networks) model each manufacturing stage as a node in a cost-aware DAG. They pick which node to evaluate next ([L745–760](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/genesis-mission-proposal/flaime-genesis-proposal.tex#L745-L760); [README](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/ecac08e/README.md)).
- **What the benchmarks actually show.**
  - The synthetic benchmark gives about **3.8× less spend than tiered screening only when CALPHAD misses an effect**, and about level when it doesn't. "No stored run gets near 10×" ([#283, Nov 25](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/283#issuecomment-7175749704)).
  - The clocked benchmark gives 2.5–3× sooner at 2–2.5× *more* money ([#283, Nov 26](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/283#issuecomment-7183681548); [PR 297](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/297)).

### Where CALPHAD fits
- **The screen.** CALPHAD seeds the screen (hot cracking and primary Al₃M phases) and the printability maps for Utah builds. It runs on Thermo-Calc if the licence is settled (#141), otherwise on pycalphad ([#256](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/256#issuecomment-7123300072); [#277, Nov 27](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/277#issuecomment-7226073050)).
- **Measuring what it predicts.** A 50-row matrix maps each Thermo-Calc quantity to a measurement. Its top three for the campaign are ICP-OES plus O/N/H, Vickers hardness, and DSC/DTA ([#262, Nov 14](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/262#issuecomment-6869926637)).

### The current campaign (the near-term goal)
- **Goal.** "**PAPER DUE DATE: Feb 28, 2027.** GOAL: Discover new alloy that performs better than AlSi10Mg and uses less critical minerals than Scalmalloy." The plan is 40 atomizer runs (36 Al, 4 Cu) in about 3 months, with "For each run we will have to thoroughly clean out the atomizer" ([#277 body, Nov 24](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/277)).
- **Baselines moved up on Nov 27.** The new baselines are commercial Sc-free LPBF alloys, EOS Al5X1 (Al-Mg-Zr-Mn) and Höganäs Al-HS1 (Al-Mn-Cr-Zr). They are to be bought, printed and tested in-house. "Al5X1 is on the Sc-free front" at 385 MPa aged ([#277, Nov 27](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/277#issuecomment-7228308921)).
- **Measured baseline so far.** In-house AlSi10Mg (Utah, April) gave 228 ± 13 MPa yield, below its 233–300 MPa datasheets ([#277](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/277#issuecomment-7226073050)).
- **Copper track.** This is the ONR YIP copper/DED proposal: 630 nickel-aluminium bronze (C63000), with DED at Utah (InssTek) and Mines (FormAlloy X2). The target is 2.5 kg of usable powder: 0.5 kg for Utah and 2 kg for Mines ([#219, Oct 28](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/219#issuecomment-6475748437); [PR 117](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/117)).

## 3. Arm opportunities, ranked by how much they would speed up or de-risk the loop

**How I ranked them.** Hands-on hours removed per run, times about 20 Jan–Feb runs. Exposure of people to open powder. Fit to a ~1.5 kg payload, ~0.1 mm (claimed) arm with a parallel gripper. Readiness before January.

**Rough size of the prize.** Steps 6–8 plus charge dosing are about 3–5 h of the 10–15 person-h per run. At 20 Al runs in Jan–Feb, that is roughly **60–100 person-hours**. This is my estimate from the schedule's table, and those durations are themselves untimed.

### Contamination and handling rules that would apply to any arm cell
- **Desiccant:** never loose silica gel near powders; use 3A molecular sieve ([#130, Sep 17](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/130#issuecomment-6353363838)).
- **Hardware cross-contamination:** keep separate sieves per alloy, and a separate copper set of crucible, nozzle and sealing rod ([SOP](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/claude/issue-251-20261110-2340/SOP/atomizer-sop.md); [#234](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/234)).
- **Static:** with fine powders, PETG and other plastics "may not be the best" (outside advice on powder dispensers; [#125, Feb 4](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/125#issuecomment-6353356551)). Fine Si jammed the AutoTrickler's large auger ([#136, Mar 18](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/136#issuecomment-6353368122)).
- **Samples:** handle SEM samples with powder-free gloves and no breath or air on them (ASTM E1078 notes) ([#138, May 7](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/138#issuecomment-6353374614)).
- **Existing safety kit:** Class D extinguisher on hand since Feb 4, mounting spot still to be chosen ([#126](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/126)). Nilfisk explosion-proof vacuum since Apr 1 ([#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/128#issuecomment-6353362101)).
- **Room rules proposed Nov 18:** signs, PPE staged outside the box, a scale "in the box too for powders and metal only", and dedicated tools ([#267](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/267)).

## 6. Open questions to settle before committing the arm

1. **Placement and EHS.** Where would the arm sit: next to the 154 CB enclosure, in the powder "box", or by the EMF door? Who signs off on an unrated robot near Al powder under NFPA 484/660? The College of Engineering industrial hygienist made the Oct 8 gas ruling ([PR 190](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/190)). BYU has no combustible-dust or robot program ([PR 266](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/266#issuecomment-7051629464)). PiPER's dust tolerance is unknown and needs checking.
2. **Charge route.** Cups and powder, or sawn bar plus master-alloy pieces? This decides whether rank 3 (kitting) is worth building ([#250](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/250); [#256](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/256#issuecomment-7123300072)).
3. **Equipment the arm would tend.** Ro-Tap and the 45 µm sieve; an Archimedes kit and balance; a microhardness tester; whether an interim glovebox is bought (sgbaird favoured a used Terra Series 100 on [Sep 27](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/130#issuecomment-6353364412)). None is in place ([PR 175](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/175#issuecomment-6479961909); [#144](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/144); [#262](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/262#issuecomment-6869926637)).
4. **People.** Who would own the cell, given that `gage-erickson` and `ronnie-guymon` carry the atomizer and SEM load ([#256](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/256#issuecomment-7123300072))? Would SO-101 demonstrations by newer students fit the PI's "people as a product" aim ([#283](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/283#issuecomment-7182240987))?
5. **Data hooks.** Balance read-out, label printing and lot codes into the run record and Excel. Step timestamps for Fig. 1 and the optimizer's costs ([#256](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/256#issuecomment-7123300072)).
6. **Atomizer dependency.** If the restart slips past Dec 18, there is little Al powder to work up in January. The cell could then demonstrate on the bronze lots and the four bought baseline powders, if those are purchased ([#277](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/277#issuecomment-7228308921)).
7. **Unknown outcomes.** The Genesis Phase I proposal and ONR YIP outcomes aren't recorded in DAL. The M2 wall build (due Nov 20) has no reported result ([PR 243](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/243)).
